## Overview

Krita is a free and open source digital painting application designed for illustrators, concept artists, matte painters, and comic creators. It is part of the KDE project and offers a powerful brush engine, animation support, and a customizable interface.

## Key Features

- **Brush engine** — Over 100 professionally designed brushes with full customization
- **Vector tools** — Built-in vector tools for line art and shapes
- **Animation** — Frame-by-frame animation with timeline and onion skinning
- **Layer system** — Advanced layer system with masks, groups, and blending modes
- **Color management** — Full color management with HDR and wide gamut support
- **Drawing assistants** — Perspective grids, vanishing points, and smoothing
- **Resource manager** — Import and manage brush packs, gradients, and palettes

## Use Cases

- **Digital artists** — Illustration, concept art, and matte painting
- **Comic creators** — Comic book and manga creation with panel tools
- **Animators** — Frame-by-frame 2D animation
- **Texture artists** — Creating textures for 3D models and games

## Who It's For

Krita is designed for digital artists who need a professional-grade painting application. It is particularly strong for illustration and concept art workflows. Being free and cross-platform, it is accessible to students and hobbyists.
