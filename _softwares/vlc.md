## Overview

VLC Media Player is a free and open source media player developed by the VideoLAN project. It supports a wide range of audio and video formats, streaming protocols, and can be used as a media converter. VLC is available on virtually every platform.

## Key Features

- **Format support** — Plays MPEG, AVI, MP4, MKV, WebM, FLV, and dozens more
- **Streaming** — Stream media over network protocols (HTTP, RTSP, UDP, RTP)
- **Conversion** — Convert media between formats
- **Subtitles** — Full subtitle support including SRT, SSA, VTT, and embedded
- **Audio effects** — Equalizer, compressor, and spatializer
- **Video effects** — Adjust brightness, contrast, hue, and apply filters
- **Plugins** — Extensible through Lua scripts and plugins
- **Recording** — Record live streams and screen capture

## Use Cases

- **General users** — Playing any video or audio file without codec issues
- **Power users** — Media conversion and streaming
- **Educators** — Playing educational videos with subtitle support
- **IT professionals** — Network media streaming and testing

## Who It's For

VLC is for anyone who needs a reliable media player. It is the go-to solution when other players fail to play a file. Its cross-platform availability and format-agnostic approach make it universally useful.
