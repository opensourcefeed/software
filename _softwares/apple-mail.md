## Overview

Apple Mail has shipped as the default email client on Mac OS X (and later macOS) since 2002, and has been included on iOS since the first iPhone's launch in 2007, making it one of the most widely used email clients simply by virtue of being pre-installed on hundreds of millions of Apple devices. Unlike Outlook or Thunderbird, Apple Mail is exclusive to Apple's own operating systems, with no Windows, Linux, or Android version ever released.

Apple Mail connects to standard IMAP, POP3, and Exchange ActiveSync accounts, so it works with Gmail, Outlook.com, iCloud Mail, and most other providers without requiring a proprietary protocol, distinguishing it from ecosystem-locked clients tied to a single mail provider. It's closed source and deeply integrated with the rest of Apple's platform: iCloud Keychain for stored credentials, Spotlight search, Handoff for continuing a draft between devices, and Focus modes for filtering notifications by context. Apple has added Mail-specific privacy features over time, including Mail Privacy Protection (introduced in 2021), which hides a recipient's IP address and blocks invisible tracking pixels used to detect whether an email was opened.

## Key Features

- **Standard protocol support** — IMAP, POP3, and Exchange ActiveSync, compatible with most major email providers
- **Mail Privacy Protection** — Blocks tracking pixels and hides IP address from senders by default
- **Unified inbox** — Combined view across multiple accounts
- **Smart mailboxes** — Rule-based virtual folders that aggregate messages by custom criteria
- **Handoff** — Continue composing a draft seamlessly between Mac, iPhone, and iPad
- **VIP and Focus integration** — Priority senders and notification filtering tied to macOS/iOS Focus modes

## Use Cases

- **Mac and iPhone users** — Default email client requiring no separate download or account setup beyond adding accounts
- **Multi-provider users** — Managing Gmail, iCloud, and other IMAP accounts from one unified inbox
- **Privacy-conscious users** — Built-in tracking-pixel and IP-masking protection with no configuration required
- **Continuity workflows** — Drafting and reading email seamlessly across Apple devices

## Who It's For

Apple Mail is aimed at Mac and iOS users who want a capable, pre-installed email client tightly integrated with Apple's ecosystem. It's distributed free of charge and bundled with macOS and iOS, with no versions available for Windows, Linux, or Android.
