## Overview

Spotify launched in Sweden in 2008, founded by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon, and grew to become the largest music streaming service by paid subscribers worldwide. It popularized the freemium, ad-supported streaming model for licensed music, later expanding into podcasts and audiobooks and building out algorithmic recommendation features like Discover Weekly and annual "Wrapped" summaries.

Spotify's client and backend are closed source, and its entire catalog depends on licensing deals with record labels and rights holders rather than user-owned files. Playback requires an active Spotify account and, in most cases, an internet connection, since offline playback is limited to downloads made within the app for paying subscribers. Because the catalog is licensed rather than owned, tracks and albums can be removed from the service at any time at a label's request.

## Key Features

- **Large licensed catalog** — Tens of millions of tracks, podcasts, and audiobooks
- **Algorithmic recommendations** — Discover Weekly, Daily Mix, and Wrapped
- **Cross-device sync** — Playback state and playlists synced across devices
- **Offline downloads** — Available to Premium subscribers within the app
- **Social features** — Collaborative playlists and friend activity
- **Podcast hosting and ads** — Built-in podcast platform with Spotify-served advertising

## Use Cases

- **Casual music listening** — On-demand access to a large licensed catalog
- **Podcast listening** — Centralized playback for music and podcasts in one app
- **Discovery** — Algorithmic playlists for finding new music
- **Multi-device households** — Shared or individual accounts across phones, computers, and speakers

## Who It's For

Spotify is aimed at listeners who want convenient, on-demand access to a large catalog of licensed music and podcasts without managing their own files. It requires an account and, for full functionality, a paid subscription, which is the main trade-off for users who'd rather own their music library or run their own streaming server.
