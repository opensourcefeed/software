## Overview

Adobe Acrobat originated in 1993 alongside the PDF format itself, which Adobe created and later handed to the ISO as an open standard while keeping its own Acrobat applications closed source and proprietary. Acrobat Reader, the free viewer, remains widely installed as a baseline way to open PDFs, while editing, redaction, form creation, and e-signature features are gated behind paid Acrobat Standard and Pro subscriptions.

Acrobat is closed source and distributed by subscription: Adobe's current plans include Acrobat Standard and Acrobat Pro, priced per user per month, with Acrobat Pro adding advanced editing, redaction, and web form tools on top of Standard's core PDF conversion and e-signature features. Adobe has also begun bundling AI-assisted document summarization and Q&A tools into paid Acrobat plans.

## Key Features

- **PDF creation and export** — Convert to and from PDF, including from Office file formats
- **Text and image editing** — Directly edit text and images inside an existing PDF (paid tiers)
- **E-signatures** — Send, sign, and track documents for signature
- **Redaction** — Permanently remove sensitive text or images from a document (Pro tier)
- **Form creation** — Build fillable web and PDF forms
- **AI Assistant** — Document summarization and question-answering, bundled into paid plans

## Use Cases

- **Business document workflows** — Contracts, forms, and signature requests inside organizations already on Adobe's ecosystem
- **PDF editing** — Making direct text or layout changes to an existing PDF
- **Compliance and redaction** — Removing sensitive information from documents before sharing
- **Cross-format conversion** — Converting between PDF and Office document formats

## Who It's For

Acrobat is aimed at individuals and organizations that need PDF editing, redaction, e-signatures, or form creation and are willing to pay a recurring subscription for them. It's a weaker fit for anyone who only needs to view or lightly annotate PDFs, since that core functionality is available for free in open source tools without a subscription.
