## Overview

Slack is a proprietary team collaboration platform developed by Slack Technologies (now part of Salesforce). It organizes conversations into channels, supports direct messaging, file sharing, and integrates with a wide range of third-party services. Slack is widely used in corporate and development teams.

## Key Features

- **Channels** — Organized conversations by topic, team, or project
- **Direct messaging** — One-on-one and group direct messages
- **Threads** — Organized discussions within channels
- **File sharing** — Upload and share files with preview support
- **Integrations** — Connect with Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, Zoom, and hundreds more
- **Search** — Full-text search across messages and files
- **Workflow Builder** — Automate routine tasks without code
- **Slack Connect** — Collaborate with external organizations

## Use Cases

- **Development teams** — Code reviews, deployment notifications, and team chat
- **Marketing teams** — Campaign coordination and asset sharing
- **Remote teams** — Async communication and team updates
- **Customer support** — Internal coordination and ticket routing

## Who It's For

Slack is designed for teams and organizations of all sizes that need structured communication. It is particularly popular with tech companies, startups, and remote-first organizations. The free tier has message history limits; paid tiers unlock unlimited history and advanced features.
