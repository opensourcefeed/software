## Overview

Camtasia is a screen recording and video editing application developed by TechSmith, a software company based in Okemos, Michigan. It pairs a screen recorder with a dedicated, timeline-based video editor built specifically for tutorial and training content, including features like automatic callouts, cursor effects, and quizzing for e-learning. Camtasia is sold as paid, proprietary software with a free trial, and runs on Windows and macOS; it has no Linux build.

## Key Features

- **Screen recording** — Captures the full screen, an application window, or a region, along with webcam and system or microphone audio
- **Dedicated video editor** — A timeline-based editor built around screen-recorded footage, with tracks for video, audio, and callouts
- **Callouts and annotations** — Pre-built shapes, text boxes, arrows, and highlights for annotating software walkthroughs
- **Cursor and click effects** — Automatic cursor smoothing, click sounds, and keystroke display for cleaner tutorial recordings
- **Quizzing** — Interactive quizzes that can be embedded in exported videos for e-learning
- **Templates and themes** — Pre-built intro/outro templates and consistent color themes across projects

## Use Cases

- **Software tutorials** — Recording and editing step-by-step walkthroughs of applications
- **Corporate training** — Producing training videos with quizzes and branded templates
- **Course creation** — Building e-learning content for platforms that support interactive video
- **Presentation recording** — Recording and editing narrated slide presentations

## Who It's For

Camtasia targets trainers, educators, and software companies producing polished tutorial and e-learning video, where its purpose-built callouts, quizzing, and templates save editing time compared with a general-purpose video editor. It's a Windows/macOS-only, paid product, which rules it out for Linux users or anyone who wants to inspect or modify the software they run.
