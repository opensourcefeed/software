## Overview

Adobe Lightroom was released in 2007, built specifically around the non-destructive RAW photo editing and cataloging workflow that professional and enthusiast photographers needed, distinct from Photoshop's pixel-level compositing and retouching focus. Rather than editing an image's pixels directly, Lightroom applies edits as a stack of reversible instructions layered on top of the original RAW file, so adjustments can be tweaked or removed at any point without ever altering the source image.

Adobe currently distributes two related products under the Lightroom name: Lightroom Classic, a desktop-only application with a local file-based catalog aimed at photographers managing large image libraries, and Lightroom (formerly Lightroom CC), a cloud-native version that syncs a photo library across desktop, mobile, and web with Adobe's Creative Cloud storage. Both are closed source and available only through a Creative Cloud subscription (either a standalone Lightroom plan or the Photography plan bundling Lightroom and Photoshop), with no perpetual-license or free option beyond a limited trial.

## Key Features

- **Non-destructive editing** — Every adjustment is stored as editable metadata, leaving the original RAW file untouched
- **Catalog management** — Library-wide organization with keywords, star ratings, collections, and smart-search filters
- **RAW processing** — Wide camera and lens profile support for RAW file development
- **AI-powered masking** — Subject, sky, and object-aware selective adjustments using Adobe's Sensei/Firefly AI
- **Cloud sync (Lightroom)** — Photo library synced across desktop, mobile, and web via Creative Cloud
- **Presets** — Reusable one-click edit presets, including a large third-party preset marketplace

## Use Cases

- **Professional photography** — Batch editing and culling large shoots efficiently
- **Photo cataloging** — Organizing and searching large personal or professional photo libraries
- **Mobile and travel editing** — Editing and syncing photos from a phone via the cloud-native Lightroom app
- **Print and web export** — Preparing color-corrected images for print or online publishing

## Who It's For

Adobe Lightroom is aimed at photographers who need non-destructive RAW editing paired with library-wide cataloging tools, from hobbyists to working professionals. It requires an active Creative Cloud subscription, with no perpetual license or self-hosted option, and Lightroom Classic's local catalog is desktop-only (Windows and macOS), while the cloud-native Lightroom app also supports Android and iOS.
