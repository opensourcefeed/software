## Overview

Tenacity was created in 2021 by a group of Audacity contributors and users after Muse Group, which had recently taken over Audacity's development, proposed adding telemetry and error-reporting features and considered relicensing the project. Tenacity forked from Audacity's codebase at that point, keeping the GPL license and removing the disputed telemetry proposals, positioning itself as a privacy-first continuation of the classic Audacity experience.

Because it shares its origins with Audacity, Tenacity retains a very similar interface and core feature set — multitrack editing, effects, and broad format support — while development has focused on modernizing the codebase (moving toward a wxWidgets-free architecture) and adding features driven directly by community requests rather than a commercial parent organization.

## Key Features

- **No telemetry** — No network calls, update checks, or usage data collection by default
- **Multitrack editing** — The same core multitrack recording and editing workflow as Audacity
- **Effects and noise reduction** — Inherited built-in effects, cleanup, and restoration tools
- **Community-driven development** — Feature decisions made by volunteer contributors rather than a commercial entity
- **Format support** — Import/export of common audio formats via bundled codec libraries

## Use Cases

- **Privacy-conscious audio editing** — Editing and recording audio without any network telemetry
- **Podcast and voiceover editing** — The same recording and cleanup workflow familiar to Audacity users
- **Migrating from Audacity** — A near drop-in replacement for users wanting to avoid Audacity's current governance

## Who It's For

Tenacity is aimed at existing Audacity users who want the same familiar editing workflow but are specifically concerned about telemetry or the direction of Audacity's current ownership. It's a natural fit for anyone who wants a privacy-first, community-governed alternative without having to learn a new interface.
