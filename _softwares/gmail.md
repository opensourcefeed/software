## Overview

Gmail launched in beta on April 1, 2004, initially invitation-only, and stood out at the time for offering a then-unprecedented 1 GB of free storage when competing webmail services measured free quotas in megabytes. It exited beta status in 2009 and has since grown into one of the most widely used email services worldwide, both as a free consumer product and as the underlying mail platform for Google Workspace (formerly G Suite) business and education accounts.

Gmail is closed source and runs entirely on Google's infrastructure; there is no self-hosted or on-premises version. Its web interface introduced conversation-style message threading and powerful search as core parts of the email experience rather than add-ons, and it integrates tightly with other Google services — Google Calendar, Google Drive attachments, Google Meet video calls, and Gemini-powered smart compose and summarization features. Free accounts are ad-supported (with contextual rather than content-scanned ads since a 2017 policy change) and subject to a shared storage quota with Google Drive and Google Photos, while Google Workspace plans remove ads and add larger quotas plus administrative controls.

## Key Features

- **Conversation threading** — Related messages grouped into a single expandable thread
- **Powerful search** — Advanced search operators and filters across a large mailbox
- **Gemini integration** — AI-assisted smart compose, summarization, and reply suggestions
- **Google Workspace integration** — Native access to Calendar, Drive, and Meet from within the mail interface
- **Spam and phishing filtering** — Machine-learning-based filtering widely regarded as effective
- **Offline mode** — Limited offline access via a browser extension or PWA

## Use Cases

- **Personal email** — Free webmail with a large storage quota and effective spam filtering
- **Business email** — Gmail via Google Workspace for organizations with custom domains
- **Cross-device access** — Web, desktop browser, and mobile app access tied to a Google account
- **Integrated scheduling** — Email combined with Calendar and Meet for a unified workflow

## Who It's For

Gmail is aimed at individuals and organizations already using or willing to adopt Google's ecosystem for email, calendaring, and file storage. It's distributed on a freemium model with a limited free tier and paid Google Workspace plans for custom domains and larger storage; all mail is hosted exclusively on Google's servers with no self-hosting option.
