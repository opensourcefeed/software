## Overview

Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic, an AI safety and research company founded in 2021. It answers questions, drafts and edits text, analyzes documents and images you upload, writes and explains code, and can search the web for current information. Claude first became publicly available in 2023, and Anthropic has released several generations of its models since, offered in tiers that trade speed and cost against capability: smaller, faster models and larger, more capable ones.

People use Claude in three main ways: through the Claude apps (on the web at claude.ai, as desktop apps for macOS and Windows, and as mobile apps for iOS and Android), through Anthropic's API, which developers use to build Claude into their own products, and through [Claude Code](/software/claude-code/), a separate agentic coding tool for the terminal and IDEs.

Claude is proprietary: the model weights, training data, and apps are closed, and the service runs on Anthropic's infrastructure and that of its cloud partners. Using it means sending your prompts and files to Anthropic. Consumer accounts include a privacy setting that controls whether conversations may be used to improve future models, and business plans come with their own data-handling terms.

## Key Features

- **Long-document and file analysis** — Upload PDFs, spreadsheets, code, and images and ask questions about them in the same conversation
- **Projects** — Group related chats with shared instructions and reference files so Claude keeps the same context across conversations
- **Artifacts** — Generate documents, code, diagrams, and small interactive apps in a side panel you can iterate on and share
- **Web search and research** — Pull current information from the web, with citations, into answers
- **Connectors and MCP** — Connect Claude to other services and to local tools through the Model Context Protocol, an open standard Anthropic introduced in 2024
- **API access** — The same models are available to developers through Anthropic's API and major cloud platforms

## Use Cases

- **Writers and analysts** — Drafting, summarizing, and editing long documents and reports
- **Developers** — Explaining, writing, and reviewing code in chat, or through Claude Code and the API
- **Researchers and students** — Working through papers, datasets, and web sources with cited answers
- **Teams** — Shared projects and organization-wide administration on Team and Enterprise plans

## Who It's For

Claude suits people who want a capable general-purpose assistant without running anything themselves and are comfortable sending their data to a cloud service. It has a free plan with usage limits; Pro and Max subscriptions raise those limits for individuals, and Team and Enterprise plans add administration and data controls for organizations. If keeping prompts on your own hardware matters more than using Anthropic's models, a local, open source assistant is the alternative.
