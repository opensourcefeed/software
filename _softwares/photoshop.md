## Overview

Adobe Photoshop is a raster graphics editor developed by Adobe Inc. It is the industry standard for photo editing, digital painting, and image compositing. First released in 1988, Photoshop has become synonymous with image manipulation.

## Key Features

- **Layer-based editing** — Non-destructive editing with layers, masks, and adjustment layers
- **Brush engine** — Extensive brush customization for digital painting
- **Selection tools** — Advanced selection tools including subject selection and object selection
- **Filters and effects** — Extensive library of filters including neural filters powered by Adobe Sensei
- **RAW editing** — Built-in Camera Raw plugin for processing RAW photos
- **Generative AI** — Generative Fill and Generative Expand using Adobe Firefly

## Use Cases

- **Photographers** — Photo retouching, color correction, and RAW processing
- **Graphic designers** — Creating marketing materials, web assets, and composites
- **Digital artists** — Illustration and matte painting
- **Web designers** — UI mockups and web graphics

## Who It's For

Photoshop is aimed at professional photographers, graphic designers, digital artists, and anyone who needs advanced image manipulation capabilities. It is available via Adobe Creative Cloud subscription.
