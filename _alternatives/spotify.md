Switching from Spotify to an open source alternative means streaming a library you actually own rather than one a company licenses and can alter or restrict at any time. Navidrome is a free, self-hosted music server that streams your own audio files through a modern web player and Subsonic-compatible mobile apps. It has no built-in catalog or discovery engine of its own, so it suits listeners who already have a personal collection of ripped or purchased music rather than those looking for Spotify's on-demand access to nearly any track.
