Adobe Audition is only available through a paid Creative Cloud subscription, with no free tier and no source code published, so there's no way to keep using it without an ongoing payment or to inspect what it's actually doing. Audacity is the most direct substitute for Audition's waveform editing and restoration workflow, with a similar noise-reduction and cleanup toolset built in. Ardour covers Audition's multitrack recording and mixing side for users doing full production sessions, while Tenacity offers the same familiar Audacity-style workflow for anyone specifically concerned about telemetry or governance. Qtractor rounds out the options for Linux users already working within a JACK-based studio setup.
