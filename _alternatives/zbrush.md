Switching from ZBrush to an open source alternative removes licensing cost while still providing dynamic topology sculpting and multi-resolution detail work for organic modeling. Open source 3D suites bundle sculpting tools alongside modeling, texturing, and rendering, so a single free application can cover both sculpting and the rest of a production pipeline rather than requiring a dedicated sculpting-only license. For most character and creature sculpting work, this alternative covers ZBrush's core capabilities at no cost, though very high-resolution production sculpts may still favor ZBrush's specialized pixol-based engine.
