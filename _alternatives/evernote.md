Switching from Evernote to an open source alternative removes subscription costs and free-tier device limits while keeping notes in an open, portable format instead of a proprietary database. Open source note-taking apps support end-to-end encryption, a choice of sync providers instead of a single vendor's servers, and typically offer a direct import path for existing Evernote notebooks. For most personal note-taking, web clipping, and organization workflows, this alternative covers Evernote's core capabilities at no cost.
