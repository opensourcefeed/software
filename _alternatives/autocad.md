Switching from AutoCAD to an open source alternative removes the recurring subscription cost while keeping DXF-based file interchange with existing AutoCAD workflows, though native DWG support may require additional plugins or format conversion. Open source CAD tools range from full parametric 3D modelers to lightweight 2D drafting applications, so the right pick depends on whether the work is mechanical part design or straightforward technical drawing. For most 2D drafting and parametric mechanical design work, these alternatives cover AutoCAD's core capabilities at no licensing cost.
