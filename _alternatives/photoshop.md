Switching from Adobe Photoshop to an open source alternative eliminates subscription costs while giving you full control over your software. Open source image editors respect your privacy, run on all major platforms including Linux, and can be customized or extended without restrictions. For most photo editing and digital art workflows, these alternatives provide comparable capabilities at zero cost.
