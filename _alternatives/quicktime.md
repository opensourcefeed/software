Switching from QuickTime Player to an open source alternative gives you far wider format support — playing MKV, FLAC, and other formats QuickTime cannot handle — on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Open source media players like VLC and mpv are free of cost, free of vendor lock-in, and receive active community updates without being tied to Apple's release cycle.
