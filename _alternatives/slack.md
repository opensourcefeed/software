Switching from Slack to an open source alternative gives you data sovereignty, end-to-end encryption, and freedom from subscription costs. Open source messaging platforms can be self-hosted, meaning your team's conversations stay on infrastructure you control. They also support open standards like the Matrix protocol, enabling federation and interoperability with other services without vendor lock-in.
