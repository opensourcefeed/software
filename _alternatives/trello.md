Switching from Trello to an open source alternative removes the free-tier board caps and Butler automation limits, and keeps your team's task data on infrastructure you control instead of Atlassian's servers. Wekan mirrors Trello's board, list, and card model on a mature, fully MIT-licensed Meteor-based codebase, and includes a direct Trello import tool, so migrating an existing team over doesn't mean relearning a new interface. Planka takes a more modern, React-based approach that visually and interactively resembles Trello even more closely, but its licensing needs a precise callout: since its 2.0 release, Planka's Community edition runs under a proprietary, non-OSI "PLANKA Community License" rather than a true open source license — free to self-host for personal or internal use, but not for commercial hosting or resale without a paid Pro/Enterprise license. If a fully open source, unrestricted license matters more than pixel-perfect Trello styling, Wekan is the safer choice; if you want the closest look-and-feel to Trello and can live within the Community license's personal/internal-use terms, Planka is worth considering.
