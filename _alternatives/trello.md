Switching from Trello to an open source alternative removes the free-tier board caps and Butler automation limits, and keeps your team's task data on infrastructure you control instead of Atlassian's servers. Wekan mirrors Trello's board, list, and card model closely enough that it includes a direct Trello import tool, so migrating an existing team over doesn't mean relearning a new interface — just self-hosting the same workflow.
