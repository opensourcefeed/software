Switching from ExpressVPN to an open source alternative means trading a closed source app and a company owned by an adware-linked parent for client code you (or an independent researcher) can actually read. Mullvad matches ExpressVPN's speed-focused, no-frills approach while going further on anonymity — no email or personal details are required to sign up, and its GPL-3.0 clients are independently audited. Proton VPN offers a similar open source, audited client with the added benefit of a genuinely usable free tier, backed by Proton AG's broader privacy-focused ecosystem. Both trade some of ExpressVPN's marketing polish and guaranteed streaming reliability for verifiable, auditable client behavior.
