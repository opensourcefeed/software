NordVPN has published the source code of its Linux app, but its Windows, macOS, and mobile apps are closed source, so on most devices you're taking its privacy claims on trust. Proton VPN and Mullvad publish the code of their apps on every platform, and both have had them independently audited. Proton VPN pairs that transparency with a genuinely usable free tier, while Mullvad goes further on the account side, requiring no email or personal details to sign up, and charges one flat monthly price instead of the multi-year discount deals common among larger commercial VPN providers.
