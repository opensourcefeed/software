Switching from NordVPN to a provider with open source clients means you're no longer taking a company's privacy claims purely on faith — the app code that handles your traffic is published, auditable, and has been independently reviewed by third-party security firms. Proton VPN pairs that transparency with a genuinely usable free tier, while Mullvad goes further on the account side entirely, requiring no email or personal details to sign up at all. Both avoid the aggressive multi-year discount pricing structures common among larger commercial VPN providers.
