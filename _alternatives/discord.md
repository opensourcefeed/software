Switching from Discord to an open source alternative gives a community control over its own infrastructure instead of depending entirely on a single company's servers staying up and its terms of service staying the same. Element, built on the federated Matrix protocol, offers rooms and spaces comparable to Discord's servers and channels, along with end-to-end encrypted voice and text and the option to self-host. It doesn't yet match Discord's bot ecosystem or gaming-focused polish, but it removes the vendor lock-in that comes with a fully proprietary chat platform.
