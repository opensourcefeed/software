NoMachine's free tier covers personal use, but commercial deployments require a paid Enterprise subscription, and the client and server are closed source. RustDesk offers a directly comparable self-hosted remote-access experience under an open source license, with no per-seat licensing if you run your own relay server. Remmina takes a different approach as a multi-protocol client for connecting to existing RDP, VNC, SSH, or SPICE servers, which suits users who need to reach machines that already expose one of those standard protocols rather than replacing NoMachine's own client-server model outright.
