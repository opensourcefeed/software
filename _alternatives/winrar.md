WinRAR keeps working indefinitely after its evaluation period, but it never stops asking you to buy a $29 license, and it's closed source Windows-only software. 7-Zip and PeaZip are both fully free, open source archivers with no reminder screens, no trial periods, and no hidden costs — and both compress at least as well as WinRAR using the open 7z format. 7-Zip is the more minimal, Windows-first option with command-line ports for Linux and macOS, while PeaZip ships a full native GUI on Windows, Linux, macOS, and BSD, along with extra file-management and secure-deletion tools.
