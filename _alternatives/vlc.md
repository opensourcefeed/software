VLC Media Player is already open source and free, but its interface and architecture date back decades — some users prefer a leaner player with better GPU acceleration, a more modern UI, or tighter Wayland integration on Linux. The alternatives listed here are all actively maintained, fully open source, and cover everything from a scriptable CLI-first player to polished GNOME and KDE-native frontends.
