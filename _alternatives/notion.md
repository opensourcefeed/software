Switching from Notion to an open source alternative means your notes stop living exclusively inside a company's proprietary cloud database. Logseq stores everything as plain Markdown files on your own device, favoring an outliner and knowledge-graph style over Notion's structured databases. Anytype keeps Notion's familiar block-and-database editing model but moves storage local-first, syncing peer-to-peer or through self-hosted infrastructure instead of a mandatory central server. Both trade some of Notion's polished templates and collaboration features for data ownership and offline access.
