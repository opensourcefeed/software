Switching from Dropbox to an open source alternative removes recurring subscription costs and lets you keep files on infrastructure you control instead of a third-party server. Open source file sync tools range from self-hosted platforms with a full web interface and app ecosystem to lightweight peer-to-peer sync with no central server at all, so you can choose the model that fits your privacy and hosting needs. For most personal and team file-sharing workflows, these alternatives cover Dropbox's core sync and share capabilities at no licensing cost.
