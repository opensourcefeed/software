A good media player should just play the file you give it, without hunting for missing codecs or nagging you with ads. Open source players have led on this for years — VLC in particular built its reputation on decoding almost anything with zero configuration, and the ecosystem now includes lighter, more specialized players too.

The players below range from VLC's do-everything approach to mpv's minimal, scriptable design, plus GTK/Qt-native players like Celluloid, Clapper, and Haruna built for Linux desktop environments, and IINA for a modern macOS-native experience.
