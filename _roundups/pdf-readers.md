Reading, annotating, and signing PDFs doesn't require a recurring Adobe Acrobat subscription — a small set of mature open source tools covers everyday viewing, quick reading, and handwritten markup without one. Okular, KDE's universal document viewer, is the broadest of the three: it reads PDFs alongside ePub, comics, and several other formats, and adds full annotation and digital signature support in the same app.

Sumatra PDF takes the opposite approach, aiming for the smallest and fastest Windows-only viewer possible rather than a feature-rich suite — a good fit for anyone who just wants a PDF open in under a second. Xournal++ is built specifically for pen and tablet input, layering handwritten notes and markup directly over a PDF background, making it the natural choice for anyone annotating documents with a stylus rather than a mouse and keyboard.
