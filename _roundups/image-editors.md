Open source image editors have matured into serious tools for photo editing, digital painting, and vector illustration, with no subscription fees and full control over your files and workflow. Whether you're retouching photos, drawing illustrations, or processing RAW camera files, there's a mature open source option built for the job rather than a single tool trying to do everything.

The editors below span different use cases: GIMP and Krita cover raster editing and digital painting, Inkscape handles vector illustration, and darktable and RawTherapee focus on non-destructive RAW photo processing. All are actively maintained, cross-platform, and free to use commercially.
